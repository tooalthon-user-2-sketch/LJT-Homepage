Hello, I'm Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working in the HKUST NLP Group under Professor Junxian He. I combine natural language processing, machine learning, and information retrieval to train large language models, using both natural-language pattern prompting and enhanced learning skills across deep learning architectures. My mission is to unify perception, cognition, and reasoning within modern AI capabilities.

## Research Interests

- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models
- LLM reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability

## Education

- **[Ph.D. candidate]**, Computer Science, at the HKUST NLP Group (2024–present).
