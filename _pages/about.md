I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science (2024-Present) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. (2020-2024) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was a recipient of the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**.
