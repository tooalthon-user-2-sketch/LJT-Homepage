I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkunlp.github.io/), supervised by Professor [Junxian He](https://jhxu.me/).

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with a particular interest in **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**, **hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)**, and **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**.

I graduated with a B.Eng. from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024.

## Research Interests

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

## Publications

I have published several papers as the first author:

1. **On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs** — EMNLP 2024
2. **SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond** — 2025
3. **On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding** — 2025

I have also co-authored papers at NeurIPS 2023 and ICML 2024.
