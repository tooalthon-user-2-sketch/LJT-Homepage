## Publications

### 1. SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond
**Junteng Liu**, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He. (2025)

**Summary**: A large-scale framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data to train models on logical reasoning tasks. We introduce SynLogic to automatically generate high-quality reasoning data by combining symbolic logic with natural language, enabling models to learn structured reasoning beyond pattern matching.

### 2. On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding
**Junteng Liu**, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He. (2025)

**Summary**: This work investigates why vision-language models struggle to accurately understand charts. We identify a perception bottleneck and propose novel solutions to enable VLMs to better extract information from charts, tables, and infographics.

### 3. On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs
**Junteng Liu**, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He. (2024)
*Accepted at [EMNLP 2024](https://2024.emnlp.org/)*

**Summary**: This paper studies the internal representations of LLMs to identify a "universal truthfulness hyperplane" — a geometric boundary in the model's representation space that separates truthful from untruthful responses. Our analysis reveals that truthfulness is not randomly distributed but follows a structured pattern that can be leveraged for better alignment.
