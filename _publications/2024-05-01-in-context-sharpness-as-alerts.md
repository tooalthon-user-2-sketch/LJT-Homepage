This paper explores using in-context sharpness of internal representations as a signal for hallucination detection. We demonstrate that sharpness in the model's internal representations can serve as effective alerts for mitigating hallucinations, offering an inner representation perspective on hallucination phenomena.

Co-authors: Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He.

*Accepted at ICML 2024.*
