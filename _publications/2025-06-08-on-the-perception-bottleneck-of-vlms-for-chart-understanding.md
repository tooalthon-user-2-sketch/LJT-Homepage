This work investigates why vision-language models struggle to accurately understand charts. We identify a perception bottleneck and propose novel solutions to enable VLMs to better extract information from charts, tables, and infographics. The study was conducted during my research internship at Tencent WXG under the supervision of Zifei Shan.

*Co-authors: Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He.*
