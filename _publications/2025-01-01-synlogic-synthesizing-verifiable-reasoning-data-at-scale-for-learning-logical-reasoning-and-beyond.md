**SynLogic** is a large-scale framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data to train models on logical reasoning tasks. We introduce SynLogic to automatically generate high-quality reasoning data by combining symbolic logic with natural language, enabling models to learn structured reasoning beyond pattern matching. This work was developed as a first-author publication with the HKUST NLP Group.

*Co-authors: Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He.*
