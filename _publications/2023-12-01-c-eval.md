C-Eval is a comprehensive multi-level and multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. The benchmark covers 13 subjects across 52 academic disciplines, providing a rigorous evaluation framework for Chinese capability evaluation.

Co-authors: Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He.
