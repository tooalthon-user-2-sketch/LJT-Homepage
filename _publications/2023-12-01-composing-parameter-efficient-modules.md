This work introduces a method for composing parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations, enabling more flexible and expressive architecture search. The approach allows combining arbitrary modules through simple arithmetic operations on their parameters.

Co-authors: Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junxian He.

*Accepted at NeurIPS 2023.*
