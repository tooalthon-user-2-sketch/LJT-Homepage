This paper studies the internal representations of LLMs to identify a "universal truthfulness hyperplane" — a geometric boundary in the model's representation space that separates truthful from untruthful responses. Our analysis reveals that truthfulness is not randomly distributed but follows a structured pattern that can be leveraged for better alignment.

Co-authors: Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He.

*Accepted at EMNLP 2024.*
